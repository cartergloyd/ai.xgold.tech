Privacy policy.

What we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Who we are.

xGold Tech ("xGold", "we") builds and runs AI workflows for business clients. This policy covers xgold.tech, the assessment request form, the customer portal and the xGold AI gateway.

What we collect.

When you send an assessment request

Your name, role (optional), work email, company, your immediate constraint, the workflow you have in mind, what it costs you today (optional), and whether it has a business owner and whether you want the scoping questions. We use these only to reply and prepare for your call. They are stored in our own database and copied into our CRM. We do not add you to a marketing list and we do not share your details.

When you visit the site

We count visits with Umami, which we host ourselves. It records the pages you view, where you came from, and your approximate location and device type. A first-party cookie named xg_vid (a random ID, kept for a year) lets us recognize the same browser when it returns. If you then send an assessment request, we link that browser's visits to your request, so we can see which pages you read first.

If you use the customer portal or the AI gateway

Your login email, a hashed password, hashed access keys, and usage totals: requests, tokens, models and the amounts invoiced. Your prompts and replies pass through to Anthropic, which provides the model. The gateway records token counts for billing and is not built to store the content of your prompts or replies.

Your choices.

  • Use "Stop tracking this browser" in the footer. It deletes the cookie and stops all visit counting for this browser.
  • Browsers that send Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track get no cookie and are counted anonymously only.
  • To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, send a message through the contact form and say so. We reply within one business day.

Who else handles data.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and reached through Cloudflare, so both see ordinary connection data such as IP addresses. Anthropic processes requests sent through the AI gateway under its own terms. Our servers, database, analytics and CRM are run by us. We do not sell personal information.

How long we keep it.

We keep a request, and the visits linked to it, for as long as we need them to run the conversation and the work that follows, then delete them. Usage records and invoices are kept as long as the law and our accounts require.

Security.

Access to our systems is limited to xGold staff. Passwords and access keys are stored only as hashes. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise it.

Changes and contact.

If this policy changes we update the date above. Questions go through the contact form. This site is for businesses and is not directed at children.
