Plan for the time, money and effort.

Three phases. Each is priced before it starts and ends in a decision you make with evidence in hand. Scope, responsibilities, costs and controls are on the How we work page.

How we work

Phase 1 · Weeks 1 to 3

Scoping assessment.

Fixed fee, quoted before we start and credited toward the build. We inventory current AI use, map one to three workflows, baseline cost and quality, and compare non-AI alternatives.

  • Current-state process map and baseline
  • Future-state workflow, permission model and test cases
  • Implementation estimate and stop/go recommendation
What we need from you
A process owner for about two hours a week, read access to the systems involved, one finance contact.

Phase 2 · Weeks 4 to 12

Build and pilot.

Scoped and priced from the assessment. We build the smallest production-relevant version, connect it, instrument every step and run it on real work with the people who own it.

  • Working pilot in the live workflow, with review queue and logging
  • Approved-use rules and role-based training
  • Acceptance scorecard against the baseline
What we need from you
A sponsor who can approve go-live, five to ten people to run the pilot, IT for access provisioning.

Phase 3 · Monthly

Operate and decide.

A monthly retainer sized to run cost and review load. We track outcomes, exceptions, human review and spend against the baseline, then expand, improve or stop.

  • Operating dashboard and finance-validated benefits
  • Monthly decision review
  • Expansion plan for the next workflow
What we need from you
The owner at one review a month. Nothing else.
